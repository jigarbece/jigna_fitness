Jigna's plan logic

Weight reduction & sustained energy: This plan targets sustainable weight loss (0.5–1 kg/week) through low GI foods, high protein, and strategic portions—while keeping energy high for your busy schedule. 7 AM is tiffin prep. Breakfast at 7:45 AM, lunch at 12:30 PM, dinner at 7:30 PM. Zero refined sugar or white flour. Water target: 3–3.5 L/day for metabolism & blood glucose control.
🍽️ 7 AM: Tiffin for son: Nutritious, quick meals you prepare. High protein keeps him satisfied.
🍴 Breakfast 7:45 AM: Low GI whole grains + high protein (paneer, tofu, curd)—sustained energy through lunch without crashes.
🥛 Protein every meal: Dal, paneer, tofu, curd, sprouts, legumes. High fiber (whole grains, vegetables) keeps you full and blood sugar stable.
🚶‍♀️ Fitness (300 min/week): 5 days cardio + strength mix (45–60 min). Weekend yoga/stretching. Proven for weight loss & metabolic health.
🫗 Hydration & snacks: 3–3.5 L water daily (crucial for metabolism). Afternoon snack at 3:30 PM (protein-rich: nuts, chana, sprouts). No processed foods.
❤️ South Indian (2x/week): Monday Idli + Sambar, Friday Dosa. Rest of week: varied rotis, dal, tofu, brown rice for metabolic variety and balance.

This is a general wellness plan, not medical care. Since you have high cholesterol, please share this plan with your doctor or registered dietitian for personalization. If you're on medication, get their OK before making changes.
